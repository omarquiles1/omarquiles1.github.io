02

Research

My current work as a research scholar in the FERES group at IUTA.

Ongoing researchIUTA · FERES

Strut aerodynamic losses in vertical axis wind turbines

The arms that join the blades of a vertical axis wind turbine to its shaft (the struts) create drag and take power away. I work with an extended double-multiple streamtube (DMST) model to estimate those losses and compare strut designs.

Research scholarship in the FERES group (Fluids Engineering for Renewable Energy) at IUTA, University of Oviedo. Ongoing research.

  • HardwareGazi University · Lab

    Synchronous buck converter with STM32

    Power electronics lab work, as a team. We sized a 15 V to 5 V synchronous step-down converter (10 W, 100 kHz), simulated it in PLECS with PI control, built it with digital control from an STM32 microcontroller and checked the waveforms on the oscilloscope.
