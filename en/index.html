About

I am a 4th-year student of the Bilingual Degree in Industrial Technologies Engineering at the University of Oviedo (Gijón Polytechnic School of Engineering). I am interested in the place where classic mechanical and industrial engineering (fluids, building services, heavy industry) meets software.

I use programming, data, simulation and AI to analyse and improve physical systems. Right now I do this as a research scholar in the FERES group at IUTA, working on the aerodynamics of vertical axis wind turbines.

I have worked in a steel plant, in a building-services engineering office and in an electronics lab in Turkey, and in October 2026 my team won Hackea Avilés. I like it when what I calculate ends up working in the real world.

02

Research

IUTA · FERES · ongoing

Strut aerodynamic losses in vertical axis wind turbines

The arms that join the blades of a vertical axis wind turbine to its shaft (the struts) create drag and take power away. I work with an extended double-multiple streamtube (DMST) model to estimate those losses and compare strut designs.

Research scholarship in the FERES group (Fluids Engineering for Renewable Energy) at IUTA, University of Oviedo. Ongoing research.
